On the night between November 11th and 12th, 2025, Iceland’s skies were illuminated by a truly spectacular aurora show. Shimmering waves of green, violet, and pink undulated overhead, turning the crisp darkness into a canvas of light and movement. This extraordinary performance of nature was visible across the island, offering those who witnessed it an unforgettable visual feast. As you explore the photos from this night, you’ll see just how magical Iceland can be when the northern lights reach their peak, painting memories that linger well beyond the midnight hours.
