# 10 different ways to explore the Golden Circle in Iceland

Discover 10 different ways to explore the Golden Circle within ond day. While we already published the [ultimate guide to the Golden Circle](/blog/ultimate-golden-circle-guide/), we did not provide any samples of itineraries. This is what I will be doing today, with 10 different ideas about how to visit the Golden Circle, whether you are traveling alone or whether you want us to make this a private custom tour. Some of these itineraries are already offered as [ready-to-book private day tours](/private-day-tours-iceland/), the other ones can be [tailored for you](/contact/).

Let's now dive into 10 different ways to spend a day on the Golden Circle in Iceland, keep in mind that possibilities and combinations are endless, do not hesitate to make your own itinerary. These combinations below are meant to be various, while always fitting within a day of 10 hours maximum.

_Please note: Many sites will be present in each of those itineraries, to avoid redundancy, we will not describe them all in details, you can refer to our [golden circle guide](/blog/ultimate-golden-circle-guide/) to get more details about each of them. Also, each mentioned visit to Þingvellir National Park would include the following: Start from P1: Hákið, walk all the way down to the Öxarárfoss waterfall (and come back if you are traveling on your own, otherwise our driver will pick you up). This should take around 1hour give or take._

## Golden Circle itineraries focused on sightseeing

### Golden Circle Essentials

This itinerary focuses on the 3 main attractions of the Golden Circle, and should last a total of around 6 hours. This itinerary is ideal if you are in a hurry, do not want to spend all day on the road, or typically if you are on a cruise.

On this itinerary, you will visit:

- Þingvellir National Park
- Geysir and Strokkur
- Gullfoss

The three gems of the Golden Circle.

This is an itinerary that we do offer as a [private tour, ready-to-book](/private-day-tours-iceland/golden-circle-essentials/).

### Golden Circle and culinary experiences

This itinerary will take you to all the highlights mentioned before, with a few extra stops to enjoy local food.

- Þingvellir National Park
- Laugarvatn Hot Spring quick stop to see where bread is being baked underground. (You can stop at Fontana Spa to buy some geothermally baked rye bread!)
- Efstidalur dairy farm: Today we start with dessert! Treat yourself to a delicious ice cream, possibly the best in Iceland, home made from fresh milk
- Geysir and Strokkur
- Gullfoss waterfall
- Late lunch (usually around 2:30pm) in the world-famous Friðheimar tomato farm. ALWAYS BOOK IT IN ADVANCE as it fills up quickly.

This should take you around 9 hours in total, and is perfect to spend a full day on the Golden Circle while enjoying amazing farm-to-table food. We also offer this tour as a [private tour](/private-day-tours-iceland/golden-circle-complete-farm-to-table/)

### Golden Circle and 4 waterfalls

If you are a waterfall lover, this itinerary is for you. It will take you through no less than 4 waterfalls, while still visiting other highlights!

- Öxarárfoss in Þingvellir National Park
- Brúarfoss
- Geysir and Strokkur
- Gullfoss
- Faxafoss
- Kerið volcanic crater

This itinerary will take you around 8 hours. We do not offer it (yet!) as a ready to book day tour, but [contact us](/contact/) and we will tailor it for you.

## Golden circle and adventure

### The Golden Snorkel

This time, you are going on an adventure that takes you beyond sightseeing. Besides all the highlights of the Golden Circle, you will swim among the clearest waters in the world while [snorkeling in Silfra](/partner-experiences-iceland/silfra-snorkeling/)!

- Þingvellir National Park with a little twist if you are self-driving: Plan some extra time for your visit in Þingvellir. Your best way to do it (on a nice day) would be to NOT drive to parking P5 (where you should park for your later activity). Remain parked at P1, enjoy the view from up there, walk all the way to Öxarárfoss and then to the starting point of your snorkel tour. Then walk back from there to your car.
- Snorkel in Silfra (takes around 2-3 hours in total)
- Geysir and Strokkur
- Gullfoss

### Golden Circle and Snowmobiling Tour

Why not combine the itinerary of the [Golden Circle Essentials]() with a [snowmobiling tour on the second largest glacier in Europe]()? This is not only possible, it is also easily doable all year long. The secret is to keep it simple and stick to the main attractions:

- Þingvellir National Park
- Geysir and Strokkur
- Gullfoss
- Snowmobiling tour (2pm departure)
