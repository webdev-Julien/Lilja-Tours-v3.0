**Northern Lights on November 11, 2025** created one of Iceland's most spectacular aurora displays of the season. Shimmering waves of green, violet, and pink undulated across the sky, transforming the darkness into a canvas of natural light. This extraordinary performance was visible across Iceland, offering those who witnessed it an unforgettable visual experience.

## Peak Activity

Starting from midnight, the sky illuminated with intensity rarely seen. Green auroras filled the entire sky, with violet and red hues visible to the naked eye.
