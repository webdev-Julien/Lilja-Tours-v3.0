The 6-Day Private Guided Tour (August 13–18)
Day One – August 13
Activity: Glymur Waterfall Hike (around 3 hours, includes river crossings).
Visits: Grábrók Crater, Deildartunguhver (Europe's most powerful hot spring), and Glanni Waterfall.
Overnight: FossHotel Reykholt.
Day Two – August 14
Activity: Drive along the Kaldidalur Highland Track.
Visits & Activities: Hraunfossar waterfalls, a small-group tour to explore a 5,000-year-old lava cave, and soaking in the Húsafell Canyon Baths.
Overnight: Hotel Húsafell.
Day Three – August 15
Activity: The classic Golden Circle tour.
Visits & Stops: Þingvellir National Park, Geysir & Strokkur, Gullfoss Waterfall, Brúarfoss, and EfstiDalur Farm (for homemade ice cream).
Overnight: Hotel Geysir.
Day Four – August 16
Activity: Reykjadalur Hot Spring Hike (a moderate trail, 3.5 km each way).
Highlights: Bathe in Reykjadalur's natural hot river.
Overnight: Hotel South Coast.
Day Five – August 17
Activity: Discovery of the Icelandic South Coast.
Visits: Seljalandsfoss waterfall + Gljúfúbrúi, Skógafoss waterfall, Sólheimajökull glacier, Dýrhólaey, and Reynisfjara.
Travel: Embark on the Hérjolfur Ferry toward the Westman Islands.
Overnight: Hotel Vestmanneyjar, Heimaey Island.
Day Six – August 18
Activity: Westman Islands Exploration.
Visits & Activities: Spot puffins (if lucky), hike to the top of Eldfell Volcano, and explore the island's unique culture and geology.
Travel: Take the ferry back to the main road, followed by a drive to Reykjavík.
Overnight: Hotel Cabin, Reykjavík.
The total price for this itinerary is 1,450,000 ISK (~11,365 USD).
