**A luxury wellness weekend in Iceland** transforms a brief layover into a rejuvenating retreat. This curated three-day itinerary combines Iceland's world-renowned [geothermal wellness culture](/blog/top-5-hot-springs-in-iceland) with [exclusive experiences](/partner-experiences-iceland/exclusive/): private [helicopter flights](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-tours/) over pristine highlands, intimate spa treatments, and Northern Lights viewing from remote volcanic landscapes.

## Day One: Arrival and Blue Lagoon Retreat Immersion

A private driver meets you at Keflavík International Airport for direct transfer to the Blue Lagoon Retreat, Iceland's most exclusive geothermal resort. While the famous Blue Lagoon attracts thousands of daily visitors, the Retreat offers an intimate sanctuary reserved for discerning travelers seeking privacy and sophistication.

![Woman bathing in the milky blue waters of the Blue Lagoon Retreat](/pictures/blog/articles/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland-retreat.jpg)

The property features private lagoon access, luxury suites with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking volcanic terrain, and exclusive spa facilities using mineral-rich geothermal waters. Spend your arrival day unwinding from travel, soaking in therapeutic waters heated naturally by underground volcanic activity, and acclimating to Iceland's unique landscape.

### Fine Dining at LAVA Restaurant

Evening reservations at LAVA restaurant provide the perfect conclusion to your first day. The establishment serves contemporary Icelandic cuisine featuring ingredients sourced from the [Reykjanes Peninsula](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-peninsula/): fresh seafood, locally raised lamb, and seasonal vegetables prepared by award-winning chefs. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame views of the surrounding lava fields as you dine.

### Northern Lights from Your Private Retreat

Winter visits (September through March) offer excellent opportunities for Northern Lights viewing. The Retreat's remote location far from light pollution creates ideal conditions for witnessing aurora borealis. We arrange [private Northern Lights tours](/private-day-tours-iceland/northern-lights-private-tour-iceland/) for optimal viewing when forecasts predict strong activity, taking you to exclusive locations away from crowds.

![Luxury vehicle under spectacular northern lights](/pictures/blog/articles/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland-northern-lights.jpg)

## Day Two: Private Helicopter Adventure Over Iceland's Highlands

![Private helicopter tour over Icelandic Highlands with dramatic volcanic landscapes](/pictures/blog/articles/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland-helicopter-1.jpg)

Your private driver transfers you from the Blue Lagoon Retreat to Reykjavík Domestic Airport for the weekend's centerpiece: an exclusive [helicopter tour to Landmannalaugar and glaciers](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-landmannalaugar-glaciers/). This 2.5-hour aerial expedition showcases Iceland's most remote wilderness, inaccessible by road during most of the year.

![Helicopter flying over colorful Landmannalaugar geothermal mountains and glaciers](/pictures/blog/articles/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland-helicopter-2.jpg)

The flight path crosses the Icelandic Highlands, passing over Þórsmörk Valley's glacial rivers, the massive Eyjafjallajökull and Mýrdalsjökull glaciers, and Hekla volcano before landing in Landmannalaugar's otherworldly geothermal landscape. Multi-colored rhyolite mountains, steaming hot springs, and vast lava fields create scenes found nowhere else on Earth.

![Aerial view of glaciers and volcanic terrain from private helicopter Iceland](/pictures/blog/articles/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland/luxury-wellnes-weekend-icelan-helicopter-3.jpg)

A 15-minute landing allows you to step onto pristine highland terrain, photograph the dramatic scenery, and experience the profound silence of Iceland's interior wilderness. The return flight passes the magnificent Háifoss waterfall, one of Iceland's tallest cascades.

![Landing in remote Icelandic Highlands during luxury helicopter tour](/pictures/blog/articles/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland-helicopter-4.jpg)

We can arrange professional photography services to document this exceptional experience. If volcanic eruptions are active on the Reykjanes Peninsula (a recurring phenomenon since 2021), we can modify the route to include aerial views of flowing lava.

![Helicopter over dramatic Icelandic landscape with mountains and valleys](/pictures/blog/articles/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland/luxury-wellnes-weekend-iceland-helicopter-5.jpg)

### Return to Relaxation

Following your aerial adventure, your driver returns you to the Blue Lagoon Retreat for an afternoon of relaxation. The contrast between morning excitement and afternoon tranquility creates the perfect wellness balance. Reflect on the morning's spectacular views while soaking in mineral-rich geothermal waters.

## Day Three: Departure Transfer

On your final morning, enjoy a leisurely breakfast before your private driver transfers you to Keflavík International Airport for your onward journey. The short 20-minute drive allows for stress-free departure timing.
