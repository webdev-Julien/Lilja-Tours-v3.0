5-Day Private Iceland Adventure (January 21–25, 2026)

Day 2 – Thursday, January 22: West Iceland - Borgarfjörður Region
Activity: Explore the stunning Borgarfjörður area.
Visits: Hraunfossar lava waterfalls, the Deildartunguhver hot spring, and Glanni waterfall.

Overnight: ION Adventure Hotel
Day 3 – Friday, January 23: Golden Circle
Activity: Discover Iceland's most famous route.
Stops: Þingvellir National Park, the erupting Geysir geothermal area, and the mighty Gullfoss waterfall.
Lunch: Friðheimar greenhouse restaurant.
Overnight: ION Adventure Hotel
Day 4 – Saturday, January 24: South Coast Classics
Activity: Journey along the dramatic South Coast.
Visits: Seljalandsfoss and Skógafoss waterfalls, the black sand beach of Reynisfjara with its impressive basalt columns, the Sólheimajökull glacier, and the village of Vík.
Overnight: Hotel Rangá
Day 5 – Sunday, January 25: Reykjanes Peninsula & Departure
Activity: En route to the airport, explore the volcanic Reykjanes Peninsula.
Possible Stops (Time Permitting): Bridge Between Continents, the geothermal areas of Gunnuhver and Seltún, and lava flows from recent eruptions.
