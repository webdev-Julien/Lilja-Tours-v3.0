**[Helicopter tours in Iceland](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-tours/)** reach places that roads do not: the top of an ice cap, the lip of a highland canyon, a crater row that was a flat field three years ago. Every flight we offer departs from Reykjavík Domestic Airport, carries up to four passengers and can now be booked two ways: a seat on a shared flight, or the whole helicopter reserved for your group. This guide covers the five routes, with prices for both options, plus the new doors-off photo flights.

## Five Routes from Reykjavík

### Reykjanes Volcanoes: 30 to 40 Minutes

![Helicopter flying over the recent eruption sites and fresh lava fields of the Reykjanes Peninsula](/pictures/blog/articles/best-helicopter-tours/best-helicopter-tours-volcanic-experience.jpg)

The shortest flight and the only one with no landing, the [Reykjanes volcanoes flight](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-volcanic-eruption/) tracks the eruption sites that have reshaped the peninsula since 2021: the Fagradalsfjall craters, the Meradalir valley filled by the 2022 flow, and the Sundhnúkur crater rows whose lava pushed up against Grindavík and the Blue Lagoon through 2026. Flowing lava is never guaranteed, but the scale of the new fields is striking in any season. Read the background in our [recent volcanic history of Reykjanes](/blog/reykjanes-volcanic-recent-history/).

Shared seat: 67,500 ISK per person. [Private helicopter](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-reykjanes-volcano-private/): 270,000 ISK for up to four.

### Waterfalls and Canyons: 80 Minutes

![Helicopter tour over the Þingvellir rift and the Skjaldbreiður shield volcano](/pictures/blog/articles/best-helicopter-tours/best-helicopter-tours-craters-and-valleys.jpg)

The [Waterfalls and Canyons flight](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-craters-waterfalls-valleys/) heads north over Hvalfjörður to Glymur, the 198-metre waterfall that hikers on our [Glymur trail](/private-day-tours-iceland/glymur-waterfall-hike/) only ever see in pieces, then circles the 9,000-year-old Skjaldbreiður shield volcano and follows its lava to the Þingvellir rift. The return crosses the steaming Hengill geothermal area. One landing of about 15 minutes is included when conditions allow.

Shared seat: 79,000 ISK per person. [Private helicopter](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-waterfalls-canyons-private/): 294,400 ISK for up to four.

### Glacier Expedition: 90 Minutes

![Helicopter landed on a glacier with passengers walking on the ice](/pictures/blog/articles/best-helicopter-tours/best-helicopter-tours-glacier-landing.jpg)

The [Glacier Expedition](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-glacier-landing/) is the quickest way to stand on an Icelandic ice cap. It flies past the cliffs of Hvalfjörður and Glymur to Þórisjökull, the glacier nearest the capital, lands for about 15 minutes, and returns along the Skjaldbreiður skyline and the Þingvellir rift. Bring proper boots: the landing is on snow or rough ground, and it is colder up there than in town.

Shared seat: 120,900 ISK per person. [Private helicopter](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-glacier-expedition-private/): 399,000 ISK for up to four.

### Hot Springs and Glaciers: 2.5 to 3 Hours, Private Only

![Helicopter flying over the colourful rhyolite mountains of Landmannalaugar and the Eyjafjallajökull glacier](/pictures/blog/articles/best-helicopter-tours/best-helicopter-tours-highlands.jpg)

The [Hot Springs and Glaciers tour](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-landmannalaugar-glaciers/) is the half-day expedition into the southern highlands. It crosses the glacier volcanoes Eyjafjallajökull and Mýrdalsjökull, drops into [Þórsmörk](/private-day-tours-iceland/thorsmork-highland-valley-hiking/), lands for 15 minutes among the steaming rhyolite mountains of [Landmannalaugar](/private-day-tours-iceland/landmannalaugar-highland-paradise/), and returns past Hekla and the 122-metre [Háifoss waterfall](/private-day-tours-iceland/viking-heritage-waterfalls/). The pilot adapts the landing spot to your wishes.

Private helicopter only: 679,600 ISK for up to four.

### Glacier Lagoon and Diamond Beach: 5 to 5.5 Hours, Private Only

![Aerial view of Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon with icebergs and the Vatnajökull ice cap](/pictures/blog/articles/best-helicopter-tours/best-helicopter-tours-glacier-lagoon.jpg)

Our longest flight, the [Glacier Lagoon and Diamond Beach tour](/partner-experiences-iceland/glacier-lagoon-helicopter-tours/), spans the whole south coast. After Þórsmörk, Landmannalaugar and three glaciers, you reach Vatnajökull, the ice cap covering 8% of Iceland, and land beside Jökulsárlón lagoon and Diamond Beach, where icebergs wash up on black sand. Up to three landings are included, and the return follows the coast over braided glacial rivers. By road, this is a two-night trip; see our [South Coast and Highlands itinerary](/multiday-tours-iceland/south-coast-highlands/) for the ground version.

Private helicopter only: 1,340,000 ISK for up to four.

## Shared Seat or Private Helicopter?

Every helicopter carries a maximum of four passengers, so even a shared flight is small, and every seat has a window. A **shared seat** is the economical choice for one or two travellers. A **private helicopter** makes sense from three passengers upward, since the price per person approaches the shared rate, and it adds three things that money otherwise cannot buy on a scheduled flight: a departure time built around your day, a pilot who can linger or change the landing spot for you, and a cabin with nobody you did not bring. Each shared tour page links to its private twin, and vice versa, so you can compare the two with one click.

## Doors-Off Photo Flights

For photographers, our helicopter partner now also offers **doors-off photo flights**: the same aircraft and pilot, with the doors removed so you can shoot straight down at lava fields, glacier edges and braided rivers without glass between you and the subject. These are private charters planned around your shot list, and warm, windproof clothing is essential. They are not bookable online; [contact us](/contact/) with your dates and the landscapes you have in mind and we will arrange the flight with the operator.

## Practical Information

All flights depart from Reykjavík Domestic Airport, 10 to 15 minutes from downtown and not to be confused with Keflavík. Arrive 30 minutes early for the safety briefing; passenger weights are requested at booking for load planning, and bookings close two hours before departure. Dress in warm layers in any season, with sturdy boots for any flight that lands. The cabin is heated and large coats can travel in the baggage compartment. Bose noise-cancelling headsets are provided.

Weather decides everything. If the planned route is closed, the pilot offers an alternative; flights can often be delayed a few hours or moved to another day; and if the operator cancels with no rescheduling option, you receive a full refund. Cancellation by the traveller costs 10% within 14 days, 50% within 7 days and 100% within 2 days of the flight (25%, 50% and 100% within 3 days for the Glacier Lagoon expedition). Summer and sunset slots sell out first, so book early.
