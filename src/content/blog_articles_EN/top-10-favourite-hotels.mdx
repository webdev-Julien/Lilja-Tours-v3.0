Our top 10 favourite hotels outside Reykjavík represent the best accommodation options for [multiday tours](/multiday-tours-iceland/) across Iceland. These properties have consistently delivered exceptional experiences through perfect location, superior comfort, genuine luxury, and impeccable service.

Whenever you travel with us on a multiday tour, these hotels are our first choices to host you. Through the years, they have proven their excellence in providing memorable stays that enhance your Iceland adventure.

Do not hesitate to also check our guide to the [5 best hotels in Reykjavík](/blog/5-best-hotels-in-Reykjavik)!

## 10. Hotel Rangá

![Hotel Rangá exterior and surroundings](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-ranga-1.webp)

Located in South Iceland along the [Rangá River](/partner-experiences-iceland/fly-fishing-west-ranga/), Hotel Rangá combines luxury with Iceland's wilderness. This boutique hotel features individually themed suites inspired by world continents, creating unique atmospheres for guests. The property's remote location offers exceptional dark sky viewing, with dedicated Northern Lights wake-up service and private hot tubs in select suites.

The hotel's acclaimed restaurant serves gourmet cuisine featuring local ingredients, while the riverside setting provides tranquil natural surroundings. With only 52 rooms, Hotel Rangá maintains an intimate atmosphere while delivering four-star service. Its position makes it ideal for exploring the Golden Circle and South Coast attractions.

![Hotel Rangá interior and amenities](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-ranga-2.webp)

## 9. Hotel Geysir

![Hotel Geysir with geothermal area view](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-geysir-1.webp)

Hotel Geysir sits minutes from the famous Geysir geothermal area in the Golden Circle. This modern hotel features floor-to-ceiling windows throughout, maximizing views of the surrounding landscapes and Haukadalur valley. The contemporary design incorporates Icelandic natural materials, creating warm interiors that complement the dramatic exterior environment.

Guests enjoy direct access to hiking trails and geothermal features, with Strokkur geyser erupting every 5-10 minutes nearby. The hotel's restaurant emphasizes seasonal Icelandic cuisine. Its central Golden Circle location makes it a strategic base for discovering Iceland's most famous sights.

![Hotel Geysir rooms and facilities](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-geysir-2.webp)

## 8. Hotel Laxá

![Hotel Laxá riverside location](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-laxa-1.webp)

Nestled in North Iceland's Mývatn region, Hotel Laxá overlooks the Laxá River, renowned for salmon fishing. This boutique property ensures personalized service and peaceful stays. The modern Scandinavian design uses natural wood and stone, reflecting the surrounding landscape's character.

The hotel provides excellent access to Mývatn's geothermal wonders, including Dimmuborgir lava formations and Námafjall geothermal area. Its restaurant serves contemporary Icelandic cuisine with emphasis on fresh fish and local game. The intimate size create a retreat atmosphere while maintaining proximity to North Iceland's attractions.

![Hotel Laxá dining and common areas](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-laxa-2.webp)

## 7. Fosshotel Núpar

![Fosshotel Núpar with mountain views](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-nupar-1.webp)

Fosshotel Núpar stands beneath Núpsstaðaskógur cliffs in Southeast Iceland, surrounded by dramatic mountain scenery. This countryside hotel balances modern comfort with traditional Icelandic hospitality. Large windows throughout the property frame views of Vatnajökull glacier and surrounding peaks.

The location serves as an excellent base for exploring Skaftafell Nature Reserve, Svartifoss waterfall, and glacier lagoons. The hotel's restaurant features buffet-style dining with local specialties, while comfortable common areas encourage relaxation. Its position along the Ring Road makes it a convenient stop for South Coast exploration.

![Fosshotel Núpar accommodations and landscape](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-nupar-2.webp)

## 6. Kerlingarfjöll Highland Base

![Kerlingarfjöll Highland Base mountain setting](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-kerlingarfjoll-1.webp)

Kerlingarfjöll Highland Base offers unique accommodation in Iceland's highland interior, accessible only during summer months. Located at 670 meters elevation within Kerlingarfjöll mountain range, this property provides various options from hotel rooms to mountain huts. The isolated location delivers complete wilderness immersion surrounded by rhyolite peaks and geothermal features.

The base camp atmosphere appeals to adventurers seeking authentic highland experiences. Guests can explore colorful geothermal valleys, hike dramatic mountain trails, and experience Iceland's untouched interior. The on-site restaurant serves hearty meals suited for active travelers. Natural hot springs nearby provide relaxation after hiking. This represents one of Iceland's most remote and spectacular accommodation options.

We highly recommend to combine a stay there with our [private hiking tour to Kerlingarfjöll](/private-day-tours-iceland/kerlingarfjoll-highland-hiking/)

![Kerlingarfjöll Highland Base facilities and trails](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-kerlingarfjoll-2.webp)

## 5. Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon

![Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon with ice views](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-glacier-lagoon-1.webp)

Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon in Hnappavellir provides one of the closest hotel accommodation to Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon. Opened in 2017, this modern property features rooms designed to maximize comfort in a remote location. The contemporary architecture complements the surrounding landscape while providing full amenities expected from upscale accommodation.

Proximity to Jökulsárlón and Diamond Beach makes this hotel ideal for glacier lagoon exploration and Northern Lights viewing. The restaurant serves quality meals featuring Icelandic ingredients, essential given the area's limited dining options. Floor-to-ceiling windows in common areas showcase incredible views. The location enables early morning glacier lagoon visits before tour groups arrive.

![Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon interior and dining](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-glacier-lagoon-2.webp)

## 4. Blábjörg Guesthouse

![Blábjörg Guesthouse in Eastfjords](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-blabjorg-1.webp)

Blábjörg Guesthouse in Borgarfjörður Eystri offers intimate accommodation in one of Iceland's most remote villages. This family-run property provides genuine local hospitality in East Iceland's dramatic fjord landscape. The guesthouse features comfortable rooms with traditional Icelandic character and modern amenities.

The location provides access to exceptional hiking, including trails to Stórurð boulder field and Dyrfjöll mountains. Borgarfjörður Eystri hosts one of Iceland's largest puffin colony, with viewing opportunities from June through August. The village's artistic community and colorful elf church add cultural interest. Blábjörg's home-cooked meals and personal service create authentic Icelandic experiences in a spectacular setting.

![Blábjörg Guesthouse rooms and surroundings](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-blabjorg-2.webp)

## 3. Hotel Búðir

![Hotel Búðir on Snæfellsnes Peninsula](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-budir-1.webp)

Hotel Búðir occupies an isolated location on Snæfellsnes Peninsula's southern coast, surrounded by moss-covered lava fields and black beaches. This boutique hotel combines historic charm with modern luxury, offering rooms in a beautifully restored property. The dramatic coastal setting provides unobstructed ocean views and proximity to the iconic black Búðir church.

The hotel's restaurant ranks among Iceland's finest, serving innovative cuisine that emphasizes local seafood and seasonal ingredients. The remote location ensures dark skies for Northern Lights viewing while maintaining access to Snæfellsnes attractions including Snæfellsjökull glacier and national park, Kirkjufell mountain, and coastal cliffs. The intimate atmosphere and exceptional service create a romantic retreat feeling.

![Hotel Búðir dining and coastal views](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-budir-2.webp)

## 2. ION Adventure Hotel

![ION Adventure Hotel modern design](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-ion-1.webp)

ION Adventure Hotel presents contemporary design in a remote location near Þingvellir National Park. This architectural landmark features minimalist Nordic design with floor-to-ceiling windows framing Þingvallavatn lake and surrounding lava fields. The property combines luxury with adventure, offering sophisticated accommodation for active travelers.

The hotel provides direct access to hiking, fishing, and the Silfra snorkeling site. The Northern Lights Bar offers panoramic views for aurora watching, while the geothermal spa uses natural hot spring water. The restaurant serves modern Icelandic cuisine with creative presentations. ION attracts design-conscious travelers seeking unique accommodation near Golden Circle attractions while maintaining distance from tourist crowds.

![ION Adventure Hotel spa and common areas](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-ion-2.webp)

## 1. Hotel Jökulsárlón

![Hotel Jökulsárlón premium location](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-jokulsarlon-1.webp)

Hotel Jökulsárlón claims our top position for its unmatched location directly facing Vatnajökull glacier. This property provides the closest possible accommodation to Iceland's most photographed natural wonder. Modern rooms feature large windows showcasing glacier views, allowing guests to watch icebergs drifting from their beds.

The hotel's position enables exclusive early morning glacier lagoon access before tourist buses arrive, creating intimate experiences with this natural marvel. The on-site restaurant serves meals while guests enjoy lagoon views. Proximity to Diamond Beach, Vatnajökull National Park, and ice cave tour departure points makes this the ultimate South Coast base. The combination of spectacular location, comfort, and access to Iceland's premier attractions earns Hotel Jökulsárlón our highest recommendation.

![Hotel Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon views](/pictures/blog/articles/top-10-favourite-hotels-jokulsarlon-2.webp)
