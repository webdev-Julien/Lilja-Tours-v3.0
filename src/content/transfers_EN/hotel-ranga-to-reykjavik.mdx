## A Scenic Journey Back to the Capital

Bid farewell to the peaceful countryside setting of Hotel Ranga and travel back to the Reykjavik greater area in comfort. Your private driver will collect you from the hotel and take you on a scenic drive northward, passing volcanic landscapes, lush farmlands, and the ever-changing Icelandic skies.

Whether you're heading to your next accommodation, catching a flight, or simply returning to the city for the final leg of your trip, this private transfer ensures a smooth and relaxing end to your South Iceland stay.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you at Hotel Ranga at the scheduled time, ready to assist with your luggage. Leaving the banks of the Ranga River behind, you'll travel northward along Route 1, watching as the dramatic South Iceland scenery gradually transitions to the approaches of the capital region.

The 1.5-hour journey offers one last chance to absorb the beauty of Iceland's southern landscapes—the distant glaciers, the moss-covered lava fields, and the occasional glimpse of Icelandic wildlife. Your driver will deliver you directly to your destination anywhere in the Reykjavik greater area.

## The Perfect End to Your Countryside Escape

After experiencing the magic of Hotel Ranga—whether you witnessed the northern lights dancing above Hekla volcano or simply enjoyed the tranquility of the Icelandic countryside—this private transfer brings your South Iceland adventure to a comfortable close.
