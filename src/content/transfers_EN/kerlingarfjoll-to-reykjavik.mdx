## From Highland Wilderness Back to the Capital

Bid farewell to the striking beauty of Kerlingarfjoll and journey back to civilisation with a private transfer to downtown Reykjavik. Retrace your path along the Kjolur highland route, soaking in the vast glacial landscapes, volcanic plains, and rugged terrain one last time before returning to the vibrant streets of the capital.

After days surrounded by colourful peaks and steaming hot springs, let your private driver handle the journey while you reflect on your highland adventure.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you at Kerlingarfjoll Highland Base at the scheduled time, ready to assist with your luggage. The four-hour journey follows the legendary Kjolur route southward, passing between the mighty Langjokull and Hofsjokull glaciers.

As you travel, the dramatic highland landscape gradually gives way to more familiar terrain—glacial rivers, volcanic plains, and eventually the coastal lowlands surrounding Reykjavik. It's a final opportunity to absorb the raw beauty of Iceland's interior before returning to the capital.

## Important Information

**Please note:** This transfer is available during summer only. Maximum 4 passengers due to highland road conditions.
