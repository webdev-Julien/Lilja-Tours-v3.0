## A Majestic Journey From the Land of Ice

Bid farewell to the ethereal beauty of Jokulsarlon and its drifting icebergs as your private driver takes you from Hotel Jokulsarlon back to Reykjavik. Retrace your path along the South Coast, savouring one last look at glacier tongues, sweeping lava deserts, and the dramatic coastline that defines this corner of Iceland.

Let the memories settle in as you travel in comfort, arriving back in the capital relaxed and with a camera full of unforgettable moments.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you at Hotel Jokulsarlon at the scheduled time, ready to assist with your luggage. The six-hour journey westward offers a final opportunity to absorb the majesty of Iceland's southeastern wilderness.

As you travel, the landscape transitions from the stark grandeur of glacier country to the dramatic black sand beaches near Vik, past thundering waterfalls, and through rolling farmlands as you approach the capital. It's a fitting farewell to one of Iceland's most spectacular regions.

## Memories to Last a Lifetime

After witnessing icebergs calving into the lagoon, walking on Diamond Beach, and experiencing the raw power of Iceland's glacial landscape, this private transfer ensures your return journey is as comfortable as it is scenic. Arrive in Reykjavik ready for the next chapter of your adventure.
