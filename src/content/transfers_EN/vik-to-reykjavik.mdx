## A Scenic Farewell to the South Coast

Conclude your South Coast adventure with a private transfer from Vík back to Reykjavík. As you leave the black sand beaches and towering sea stacks behind, enjoy one last journey past glacier tongues, lush farmlands, and thundering [waterfalls](/blog/ultimate-south-coast-discovery/)—a fitting finale to your time in this remarkable corner of Iceland.

No bus schedules or rushed connections. Your private driver will ensure you travel in comfort, arriving back in the capital relaxed and filled with memories of the south.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you in Vík at the scheduled time, ready to assist with your luggage. Leaving the dramatic Reynisdrangar sea stacks behind, you'll travel westward along Route 1, watching as the landscape transitions from volcanic black sand plains to lush farmlands and glacier-capped mountains.

The three-hour journey takes you past some of Iceland's most celebrated waterfalls, with the silhouettes of Mýrdalsjökull and Eyjafjallajökull glaciers accompanying you for much of the drive. As the capital approaches, reflect on the memories you've made on the South Coast.

## Back to the City

After exploring Reynisfjara's legendary beach, witnessing the power of the [South Coast's](/private-day-tours-iceland/south-coast-complete-adventure/) waterfalls, or simply enjoying the peaceful atmosphere of Vík, this private transfer ensures your return to Reykjavík is as relaxing as your time in the south. Arrive ready for the next chapter of your Icelandic adventure.
