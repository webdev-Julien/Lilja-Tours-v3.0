## A Smooth Journey to Your Flight Home

Conclude your South Iceland stay with a private transfer from Hotel Rangá to Keflavík International Airport. Enjoy one last scenic drive through Iceland's dramatic southern landscapes—rolling farmlands, volcanic plains, and coastal vistas—as you make your way to the airport.

No need to worry about bus schedules or connections. Your private driver will ensure you arrive relaxed and on time, with nothing but fond memories of your Icelandic adventure.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you at Hotel Rangá at the scheduled time, ready to assist with your luggage. Leaving the banks of the Rangá River behind, you'll travel westward through the beautiful South Iceland countryside, watching as the landscape transitions from lush farmlands to the dramatic volcanic terrain of the [Reykjanes Peninsula](/blog/ultimate-reykjanes-guide/).

The approximately 2.5-hour journey offers one final opportunity to absorb Iceland's incredible scenery. Your driver will deliver you directly to the departures terminal at Keflavík International Airport, ensuring a stress-free end to your Icelandic adventure.

## The Perfect Farewell

After experiencing the magic of Hotel Rangá—whether you witnessed the [northern lights](/private-day-tours-iceland/northern-lights-private-tour-iceland/) dancing above Hekla volcano, explored the wonders of the [South Coast](/private-day-tours-iceland/south-coast-complete-adventure/), or simply enjoyed the tranquility of the Icelandic countryside—this private transfer ensures your journey home begins as smoothly as your adventure started.
