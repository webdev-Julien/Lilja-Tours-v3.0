## One Last Scenic Journey

Conclude your West Iceland adventure with a private transfer from Húsafell to Keflavík International Airport. As you leave the woodland tranquility behind, enjoy one last scenic drive through the captivating Borgarfjörður valley, passing cascading waterfalls, geothermal springs, and the rolling landscapes that make this region so memorable.

## The Journey Home

Your driver will collect you from your Húsafell accommodation at the scheduled time, ensuring you have plenty of time for the 3-hour journey to the airport. The route takes you through some of West Iceland's most beautiful scenery—a fitting farewell to this enchanting corner of the country.

## Stress-Free Departure

No rushing, no connections to coordinate. This private transfer ensures you arrive at Keflavík International Airport calm, comfortable, and with plenty of time before your departure. After exploring [Langjökull glacier](/partner-experiences-iceland/into-the-glacier/), relaxing in Húsafell's geothermal pools, or discovering the region's remarkable lava caves, let your final Icelandic journey be as memorable as the rest of your adventure.
