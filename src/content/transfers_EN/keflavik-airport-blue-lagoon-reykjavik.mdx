## Combine Convenience and Relaxation on Your Arrival

Begin your Icelandic adventure in the most seamless way possible. Upon landing at Keflavík International Airport, your private driver will greet you and whisk you away to the world-renowned Blue Lagoon. Enjoy 2.5 hours of pure bliss in the milky-blue geothermal waters before continuing your journey to your Reykjavík hotel, refreshed and ready to explore.

This private transfer eliminates the hassle of coordinating multiple transports and luggage storage. Your driver will handle your bags while you soak, then deliver you directly to your accommodation in the capital.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you in the arrivals hall at Keflavík International Airport, holding a sign with your name. After collecting your luggage, you'll be driven to the Blue Lagoon, located just 20 minutes from the airport on the stunning [Reykjanes Peninsula](/blog/ultimate-reykjanes-guide/).

While you unwind in the warm, mineral-rich waters, your driver will keep your luggage safe in the vehicle. After 2.5 hours of relaxation, you'll continue your journey to Reykjavík, arriving at your hotel completely refreshed and without the stress of managing connections or bags.

## Important Information

**Blue Lagoon entrance tickets are not included** in this transfer and must be booked separately in advance. The Blue Lagoon often sells out, so we recommend securing your tickets as early as possible. Contact us if you would like assistance arranging your Blue Lagoon reservation.
