## Skip the Capital, Embrace the West

Arrive straight into West Iceland's natural paradise. Your private driver will meet you at Keflavík International Airport and whisk you directly to Húsafell, bypassing Reykjavík entirely. This 3-hour journey takes you through the striking Borgarfjörður region, where lush valleys, historic farmlands, and powerful rivers carve their way through ancient lava fields.

## Gateway to Wonders

Húsafell is perfectly positioned for exploration. The magnificent [Hraunfossar and Barnafoss waterfalls](/blog/ultimate-guide-to-the-silver-circle/) are just minutes away, while the vast Langjökull glacier offers unforgettable ice cave adventures. The region also boasts some of Iceland's most impressive lava caves, including Víðgelmir—one of the largest in the country.

## Arrive Relaxed and Ready

After your flight, the last thing you need is the hassle of coordinating connections through the capital. This private transfer ensures you arrive at your West Iceland accommodation calm, comfortable, and ready to discover this unspoiled corner of the country. Whether you're heading to Húsafell for glacier adventures, hot spring relaxation, or simply to experience the serene Icelandic countryside, your journey begins the moment you land.
