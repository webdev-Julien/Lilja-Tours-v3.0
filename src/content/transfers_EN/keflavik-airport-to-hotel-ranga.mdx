## Skip the Capital, Start Your Adventure

Skip the capital and head directly into the Icelandic countryside with a private transfer from Keflavik International Airport to Hotel Ranga. As you travel along the scenic southern coast, you'll be treated to sweeping views of volcanic landscapes, distant waterfalls, and the rugged beauty that makes Iceland unforgettable.

Your destination is one of Iceland's most celebrated countryside hotels. Set along the banks of the Ranga River with views of the legendary Hekla volcano, Hotel Ranga offers world-class comfort in the heart of nature—and some of the best northern lights viewing in the country.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you at Keflavik International Airport arrivals hall, ready to assist with your luggage. From the airport, you'll travel eastward along the scenic southern coast, bypassing Reykjavik entirely as the landscape transforms from the moonlike terrain of the [Reykjanes Peninsula](/blog/ultimate-reykjanes-guide/) to the lush farmlands and volcanic plains of South Iceland.

The approximately 2.5-hour journey offers an incredible introduction to Iceland's diverse landscapes. Watch for distant waterfalls, glacier-capped mountains on the horizon, and the ever-present sense of Iceland's geological drama. Your driver will deliver you directly to Hotel Ranga, where your countryside adventure awaits.

## Arrive Ready to Explore

Whether you're seeking [northern lights](/private-day-tours-iceland/northern-lights-private-tour-iceland/), planning [South Coast](/blog/ultimate-south-coast-discovery/) excursions, or simply craving a luxurious escape into nature, this direct airport transfer ensures you begin your Icelandic adventure relaxed and refreshed—without the hassle of navigating buses or rental cars.
