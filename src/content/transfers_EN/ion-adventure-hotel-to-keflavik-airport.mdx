## A Scenic Farewell to Iceland's Wilderness

Conclude your stay at the ION Adventure Hotel with a private transfer to Keflavík International Airport. Enjoy one last journey through the breathtaking landscapes surrounding [Thingvellir National Park](/blog/ultimate-golden-circle-guide/) and Lake Thingvallavatn before crossing the striking lava fields of the [Reykjanes Peninsula](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-peninsula/) on your way to the airport.

Relax in comfort as your private driver takes care of everything, ensuring you arrive at the airport refreshed and on time for your departure.

## The Journey

Your transfer begins at the iconic ION Adventure Hotel, nestled in the dramatic landscapes near Thingvellir. As you depart, you'll have the opportunity to take in the stunning views of Lake Thingvallavatn, Iceland's largest natural lake, and the rugged terrain that makes this region so special.

The route takes you past Thingvellir National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its geological and historical significance. From there, the drive continues across the otherworldly lava fields of the [Reykjanes Peninsula](/blog/ultimate-reykjanes-guide/), offering a final glimpse of Iceland's raw volcanic beauty.

Your driver will ensure a smooth, comfortable journey, handling all luggage and timing your arrival perfectly for your flight departure.
