## Carry the Magic Back With You

Leave the shimmering icebergs and pristine wilderness of Southeast Iceland behind as your private driver takes you from FossHotel Glacier Lagoon back to Reykjavík. Enjoy one last journey along the spectacular [South Coast](/blog/ultimate-south-coast-discovery/), passing glacial rivers, sweeping coastal views, and the dramatic landscapes that make this route one of the most scenic in the world.

Sit back, relive the highlights of your glacier lagoon adventure, and arrive in the capital refreshed and ready for the next chapter of your trip.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you at FossHotel Glacier Lagoon at the scheduled time, ready to assist with your luggage. As you leave the glacial wonderland behind, the five-hour journey westward offers one final opportunity to absorb the majesty of Southeast Iceland.

Watch as the landscape transitions from the stark beauty of glacial outwash plains to the dramatic coastal scenery of Vík and the [South Coast](/private-day-tours-iceland/south-coast-complete-adventure/). Glacier tongues give way to towering waterfalls, and volcanic black sand beaches stretch toward the horizon. It's a cinematic farewell to one of Iceland's most remarkable regions.

## A Scenic Farewell

After days surrounded by icebergs and glacial beauty, let your private driver handle the journey while you reflect on your adventure. Arrive in Reykjavík relaxed and with a camera full of unforgettable moments from the edge of the ice cap.
