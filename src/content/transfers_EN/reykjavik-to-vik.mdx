## Discover the Magic of the South Coast

Trade the vibrant capital for one of Iceland's most dramatic stretches of coastline. Your private driver will take you from Reykjavik to the charming village of Vik, passing some of the country's most iconic scenery along the way—cascading waterfalls, glacier-capped volcanoes, and vast black sand plains shaped by centuries of volcanic activity.

Nestled beneath the Myrdalsjokull glacier and home to the famous Reynisfjara black sand beach, Vik is the perfect base for exploring South Iceland's wild beauty. Relax and enjoy the journey as the landscape transforms with every passing mile.

## The Journey

Your driver will collect you from your Reykjavik accommodation and head eastward along the iconic Route 1. The three-hour journey takes you past some of Iceland's most celebrated landmarks—the powerful waterfalls of Seljalandsfoss and Skogafoss, the glacier-capped peaks of Eyjafjallajokull and Myrdalsjokull, and the dramatic volcanic landscapes that define this region.

As you approach Vik, the famous Reynisdrangar sea stacks come into view, rising dramatically from the Atlantic. Your driver will deliver you to your destination in this charming coastal village, ready to begin your South Coast adventure.

## Your South Coast Base Camp

Whether you're here to explore Reynisfjara's legendary black sand beach, venture onto a glacier, or simply soak in the dramatic coastal scenery, Vik offers the perfect base. Arrive relaxed and ready to discover why this corner of Iceland captivates visitors from around the world.
