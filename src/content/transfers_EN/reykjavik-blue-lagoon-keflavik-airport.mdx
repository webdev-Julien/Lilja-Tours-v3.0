## End Your Iceland Adventure in Pure Relaxation

Before catching your flight home, treat yourself to one last iconic Icelandic experience. Your private driver will collect you from your Reykjavík hotel and take you to the world-renowned Blue Lagoon. Spend 2.5 hours unwinding in the warm, mineral-rich geothermal waters—the perfect way to reflect on your adventure and ease into travel mode. Afterwards, your driver will take you directly to Keflavík International Airport, refreshed and ready for your flight.

This private transfer takes all the stress out of your departure day. No rushing, no coordinating buses or luggage storage—just relaxation and seamless service from door to departure gate.

## The Journey

Your driver will meet you at your Reykjavík hotel at the scheduled time. From there, you'll head to the Blue Lagoon on the [Reykjanes Peninsula](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-peninsula/), located approximately 45 minutes from the capital. While you soak in the famous milky-blue waters, your driver will keep your luggage safe in the vehicle.

After 2.5 hours of relaxation, you'll continue the short 20-minute drive to Keflavík International Airport, arriving calm, refreshed, and ready for your journey home.

## Important Information

**Blue Lagoon entrance tickets are not included** in this transfer and must be booked separately in advance. The Blue Lagoon often sells out, so we recommend securing your tickets as early as possible. Contact us if you would like assistance arranging your Blue Lagoon reservation.
