## Discover Vatnshellir Cave on Snæfellsnes Peninsula

The **Vatnshellir Cave Tour** takes you 35 meters beneath the [Snæfellsnes Peninsula](/blog/ultimate-snaefellsnes-guide/) into an 8,000-year-old [lava tube](/partner-experiences-iceland/lava-caves/). Located beneath Snæfellsjökull glacier, this cave inspired Jules Verne's "Journey to the Center of the Earth."

Your 45-minute guided exploration descends a spiral staircase through chambers showcasing volcanic power. The cave maintains 2-4°C year-round, where you witness how lava sculpted these formations over millennia. Vatnshellir is a popular addition to our [private Snæfellsnes Peninsula day tour](/private-day-tours-iceland/snaefellsnes-peninsula/). For a broader look at volcanic landscapes, browse our article on [how to see lava in Iceland](/blog/how-to-see-lava-in-iceland/).

## What Makes This Experience Special

This **[lava cave tour](/partner-experiences-iceland/lava-caves/)** explores one of Iceland's most accessible volcanic formations. Helmets with LED headlamps allow safe navigation while guides share insights into volcanic geology and the area's literary heritage—enriching for literature enthusiasts and nature lovers alike.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Professional cave guide, helmet with LED headlamp, 45-minute underground exploration, geological education and insights, connection to Jules Verne's literary legacy.

**Requirements:** Minimum age 12 years, closed-toe shoes with good grip required, warm clothing recommended due to cave temperature, basic mobility and fitness required, not recommended for those with claustrophobia.

**Good to Know:** Maximum group size of 19 people ensures an intimate experience, free cancellation available up to 24 hours before tour, year-round availability with consistent cave conditions, cave depth reaches 35 meters below surface level.
