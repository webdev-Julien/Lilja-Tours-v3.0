## Soar Above Iceland's Most Dramatic Landscapes

The **[Helicopter Tour](/blog/best-helicopter-tours): Craters, Waterfalls & Valleys** offers an unparalleled aerial perspective of Iceland's most spectacular natural wonders. Departing from Reykjavik Domestic Airport, this 80-minute flight showcases volcanic terrain, cascading waterfalls, and UNESCO World Heritage sites. Your expert pilot provides fascinating insights into the geological forces shaping Iceland.

The adventure highlights [Glymur waterfall](/private-day-tours-iceland/glymur-waterfall-hike/) (198 meters, Iceland's tallest), Skjaldbreiður shield volcano, and Þingvellir National Park where tectonic plates dramatically pull apart. A 15-minute landing in pristine wilderness allows you to step onto terrain accessible only by helicopter. Just four passengers ensures window seats and unobstructed photography for everyone.
