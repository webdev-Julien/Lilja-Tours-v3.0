## Heat, Water and Time, Traced from the Air

The **Waterfalls and Canyons helicopter tour** is an 80-minute flight through extremes: a waterfall hidden in a canyon, a volcano so old and broad it looks like a hill, and a valley where the earth's crust is literally pulling apart. It is the best mid-length option among our [helicopter tours from Reykjavík](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-tours/) for travellers who want variety rather than a single headline sight, and it includes a landing of about 15 minutes when conditions permit.

This is the shared version, priced per seat with up to four passengers and a window for everyone. The same route can be flown with the helicopter reserved for your group alone: see the [private Waterfalls and Canyons flight](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-waterfalls-canyons-private/).

## The Route: Glymur, Skjaldbreiður and the Rift

From Reykjavík Domestic Airport the helicopter heads north over **Hvalfjörður**, the Whale Fjord, which most visitors bypass in the tunnel beneath it. At its head, tucked into a narrow mossy canyon, is **Glymur**, Iceland's second-highest waterfall at 198 metres. Hikers need three hours and a river crossing to see it in pieces; from the air you see the whole drop at once. Our [Glymur waterfall hike](/private-day-tours-iceland/glymur-waterfall-hike/) is the ground-level alternative.

The route then turns inland to **Skjaldbreiður**, a 9,000-year-old shield volcano whose lava built the floor of the Þingvellir valley. Following those ancient flows south brings you over the **Þingvellir rift**, where the North American and Eurasian plates drift apart and the fissures are plain from above. The return leg crosses the **Hengill** geothermal area, steam rising from the hills that heat Reykjavík. Where the pilot lands depends on the day's weather; the aim is always the best viewpoint available. For more on the region, see our [West Iceland Silver Circle](/multiday-tours-iceland/west-iceland-silver-circle/) itinerary and our guide to the [best helicopter tours in Iceland](/blog/best-helicopter-tours/).

## Weather, Flexibility and What to Expect

Bring sturdy hiking or winter boots, since landings are often on uneven ground or snow, and dress in warm layers in any season. The cabin is heated and large coats can travel in the baggage compartment. Icelandic weather changes fast: if the planned route is closed, the pilot offers an alternative; if a short delay would help, the flight can be postponed by a few hours or to another day, subject to availability; and a full refund applies if the operator has to cancel. Scenes from this route appear in our [helicopter photo gallery](/blog/best-pictures-helicopter-private-tours/).

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Seat on a shared helicopter flight (up to 4 passengers), approximately 80 minutes of flight time, one scenic landing of about 15 minutes (weather permitting), experienced pilot with commentary, Bose noise-cancelling headsets.

**Requirements:** Sturdy hiking or winter boots for the landing, warm layers in any season, booking at least 2 hours before departure. Passenger weights are requested at booking for flight planning. Independent transfer to Reykjavík Domestic Airport, or ask us to include the flight in a private tour or transfer.

**Good to Know:** Flights operate year-round and depend on weather; alternative routes or another departure time are offered when conditions change, and a full refund applies if the operator cancels.
