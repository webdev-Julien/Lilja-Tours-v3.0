## Europe's Largest Whale Exhibition

**Whales of Iceland Exhibition** features 23 life-size models representing whale species in Icelandic waters. Located at Fiskislóð 23-25, this museum brings ocean giants indoors. The massive 25-meter blue whale dominates the space. Interactive displays, multilingual audio guides, and virtual reality experiences complement the models for comprehensive marine education.

## Life-Size Models Create Profound Understanding

The **blue whale model** (25 meters) creates visceral understanding impossible through statistics: heart the size of a small car, tongue weighing as much as an elephant. **Additional species** include humpbacks with distinctive pectoral fins, sperm whales with massive squared heads, and orcas with striking coloration. Models positioned at varying heights suggest natural behaviors, transforming static displays into dynamic scenes.

## Comprehensive Marine Education

**Interactive displays** explore whale biology through engaging multimedia: echolocation demonstrations, feeding strategy comparisons, and migration tracking. **Conservation education** addresses whaling history and contemporary challenges including ship strikes and climate change.

## Perfect Preparation and Family Appeal

The exhibition provides ideal foundation before [whale watching](/partner-experiences-iceland/whale-watching/), teaching species identification that transforms encounters. The **family-friendly approach** engages children with dramatic models, virtual reality, and hands-on stations. Many families report this as a trip highlight, particularly during poor weather.
