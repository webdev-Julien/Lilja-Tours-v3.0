## Iceland's Ultimate Dual Marine Adventure

**Whale Watching & Sea Angling Combo** marries two quintessential Icelandic experiences: traditional fishing and [spectacular whale watching](/partner-experiences-iceland/whale-watching/). This 6-hour expedition from Reykjavík's Old Harbor combines complementary activities, quiet fishing periods provide ideal wildlife observation opportunities, while catching fish alternates with encountering whales and dolphins.

The tour operates from Ægisgata 5. Professional crew with decades of experience guide both activities. All equipment provided: rods, reels, bait, and warm overalls.

## Traditional Icelandic Sea Angling

The **sea angling component** introduces traditional Icelandic fishing adapted with modern equipment. Faxaflói Bay teems with cod, haddock, pollock, and catfish. Crew demonstrates techniques from baiting through landing fish, beginners quickly grasp fundamentals while experienced anglers appreciate local knowledge.

Fishing proves productive in these nutrient-rich waters where Gulf Stream meets Arctic currents. Many participants land multiple fish. Crew provides insights into sustainable practices and demonstrates traditional Icelandic preparation: cleaning, filleting, and even cooking aboard.

## Spectacular Whale Watching Opportunities

**Whale watching** provides thrilling encounters with Faxaflói Bay's diverse marine mammals. Minke whales appear frequently; humpbacks provide spectacular sightings with acrobatic breaching. White-beaked dolphins travel in energetic pods surfing bow waves. Winter occasionally brings orcas.

Crew explains biology and migrations, helping distinguish species through visual identification tips.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Whale Watching & Sea Angling Combo for 6 hours, professional fishing equipment including rods, reels, bait, and tackle, warm waterproof overalls if needed, expert crew guidance for both fishing and whale watching, marine wildlife guide services, fish preparation and cooking instruction, sustainable fishing education, all safety equipment.

**Requirements:** Minimum age 6 years suitable for families with older children, appropriate for beginners with no fishing experience required, comfortable casual clothing with warm layers, waterproof jacket recommended, sturdy shoes with non-slip soles, gloves helpful for handling fishing lines, sunglasses and sunscreen during sunny conditions, camera with zoom lens for wildlife photography, motion sickness medication if prone to seasickness, independent transportation to Reykjavík Old Harbor departure point at Ægisgata 5.

**Good to Know:** Year-round operation from Old Harbor (Ægisgata 5). Duration 6 hours combining whale watching and sea angling. Minke whales, humpbacks, dolphins commonly spotted; occasional orcas in winter. Fish species: cod, haddock, pollock, catfish. All fishing equipment and warm overalls provided.
