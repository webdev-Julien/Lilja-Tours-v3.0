## The Private Way to Visit Sky Lagoon

**Sky Lagoon Sér Pass** is the premium admission to the capital's oceanside geothermal lagoon. "Sér" means "private" in Icelandic, and that is the difference: instead of the shared facilities of the [Saman Pass](/partner-experiences-iceland/sky-lagoon-pure-pass/), you get your own changing room with a private shower, stocked with Sky Lagoon's shampoo, conditioner and body lotion, plus a hair dryer, towel and a reusable waterproof bag for your phone. It is the pass we recommend for couples, honeymooners and anyone who wants a calm start and finish to the visit. Sky Lagoon features among the [top hot springs in Iceland](/blog/top-5-hot-springs-in-iceland/).

Everything beyond the changing room is shared with Saman guests: the 70-metre infinity edge at 38-40°C, the swim-up bar, the cave section and the complete seven-step Skjól ritual.

## Why Choose the Sér Pass

On a busy evening, the shared changing areas are where time disappears. A private room means no waiting for a cubicle, a shower to yourself before and after the lagoon, and a quiet, dry space to get ready for dinner in Reykjavík afterwards. The skincare products are the same ones used in the ritual, so the experience carries through to the moment you leave.

Private changing rooms are limited in number and tend to sell out before the shared ones, especially for sunset and evening slots. If a particular time matters to you, book early. We can also build the visit into a [private day tour](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-peninsula/) or an airport transfer.

## The Seven-Step Skjól Ritual

The **Skjól ritual** (the word means "shelter") takes place in a turf-roofed building beside the lagoon. Allow about 45 minutes, longer if you linger.

**1. Laug** – Begin in the warm lagoon at 38-40°C and let your body settle.

**2. Kuldi** – A cold plunge at around 5°C. Brief, bracing, and the step most guests talk about afterwards.

**3. Ylur** – A sauna with a floor-to-ceiling window onto the ocean. Dry heat at 80-90°C.

**4. Súld** – A walk through a corridor of cold mist to cool the skin.

**5. Mýkt** – Sky Lagoon's signature body scrub, applied yourself, to exfoliate and soften.

**6. Gufa** – A steam room at around 46°C that lets the scrub work and opens the pores.

**7. Saft** – A shot of Icelandic crowberry juice, then back into the lagoon with the skin tingling.

Done in order, the sequence is a classic Nordic hot-cold contrast that boosts circulation and leaves you unusually relaxed. Winter visitors may see the [northern lights](/private-day-tours-iceland/northern-lights-private-tour-iceland/) from the water; in summer the sun barely sets. Either way, it fits naturally into a [luxury wellness weekend](/blog/luxury-wellness-weekend-iceland/) in Reykjavík.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Sky Lagoon Sér Pass admission, private changing room with your own shower, Sky Lagoon shampoo, conditioner and body lotion, hair dryer, towel, reusable waterproof bag, unlimited access to the 70-metre oceanside infinity lagoon at 38-40°C, the complete seven-step Skjól ritual (lagoon, cold plunge, sauna, cold mist, body scrub, steam room, crowberry drink), wristband for cashless payments at the lagoon bar.

**Requirements:** Minimum age 12 years; ages 12-14 must be accompanied by an adult. Swimwear required (rental available on site). A thorough shower before entering the lagoon is mandatory, as at all Icelandic pools. Independent transportation required (car, taxi or city bus routes 4, 35 and 36); we can include the visit in a private tour or transfer on request.

**Good to Know:** Open year-round with seasonal hours, from 10:00-21:00 in winter to 8:00-23:00 around midsummer. Located at Kársnes, Kópavogur, 13 minutes from Reykjavík, with free parking. Minimum age 12; ages 12-14 with an adult. Allow 3-4 hours to enjoy the ritual at a relaxed pace. Private changing rooms are limited and sell out first on popular evenings, so book early. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your visit.
