## Iceland's Most Exclusive Overnight Adventure

**Overnight Adventure on Fjallsárlón Glacier Lagoon** delivers Iceland's most extraordinary accommodation on your [South Coast Exploration](/multiday-tours-iceland/golden-circle-south-coast/): a private night floating on a pristine glacier lagoon inside a luxury igloo. This exclusive Hut combines complete solitude with panoramic Vatnajökull views, unmatched anywhere in the world.

Glass ceiling provides 360-degree views from your double bed. Watch icebergs drift, and appreciate midnight sun in summer. Modern amenities include electricity, WiFi, kitchenette, and composting toilet.

## Floating Glass Sanctuary

The **Hut** accommodates exactly two guests, ensuring exclusive privacy. Transparent construction creates magical atmosphere, morning light illuminating glaciers, twilight painting vibrant colors, stars filling overhead views.

The **kitchenette** allows breakfast preparation while watching sunrise over Vatnajökull. Note: shower facilities are not available, and guests should have dinner before 17:30 check-in.

## Romantic Solitude Near Jökulsárlón

**Fjallsárlón** provides quieter alternative to busier Jökulsárlón, just 10 minutes away. This **adults-only experience** (minimum age 20) caters to couples seeking romance and unique adventures—perfect for honeymoons, anniversaries, or proposals.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Exclusive overnight accommodation in private Hut for 2 persons, floating igloo with panoramic glacier and lagoon views, double bed with comfortable bedding, composting toilet facilities, kitchenette with basic equipment, electricity and WiFi, 16-hour stay from 17:30 check-in to 09:00 checkout, [zodiac boat](/partner-experiences-iceland/iceberg-boat-tour/) transfer to/from hut, 24-hour daylight experience in summer.

**Requirements:** Minimum age 20 years—adults only exclusive experience, maximum 2 persons per Hut, guests should have dinner before arrival (check-in 17:30), bring personal toiletries and towels (no shower facilities available on location), warm clothing for outdoor portions of transfer, food and beverages for breakfast/snacks if desired, camera equipment for photography opportunities, book minimum 1 day in advance, weather-dependent experience may be rescheduled for safety.

**Good to Know:** Year-round operation. Check-in 17:30, checkout 09:00 (16 hours). Located on Fjallsárlón, 10 minutes from Jökulsárlón. Maximum 2 guests, adults only (20+). Floating glass accommodation with 360-degree views. Amenities: double bed, kitchenette, toilet, electricity, WiFi. No shower facilities—have dinner before arrival. Access via zodiac (summer) or 4x4 (winter). Weather-dependent; may reschedule for safety. Perfect for honeymoons, anniversaries, proposals.
