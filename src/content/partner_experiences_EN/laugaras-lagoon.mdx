## The Golden Circle's Newest Geothermal Destination

**Laugarás Lagoon** is the latest addition to Iceland's celebrated [hot springs](/partner-experiences-iceland/hot-springs/) scene, perfectly positioned minutes from the [Golden Circle](/private-day-tours-iceland/golden-circle-essentials/). This exquisite two-story lagoon is nestled among mountains overlooking the Hvítá River, creating a stunning setting where warm geothermal waters blend seamlessly with the dramatic landscape.

The facility offers a complete wellness experience: soak in the perfectly heated lagoon, take an invigorating plunge in the cold pool, or unwind in one of two saunas. The design integrates modern comfort with Iceland's natural beauty, making it ideal for couples, families with children aged 8+, groups of friends, or solo travelers seeking relaxation. Our [ultimate Golden Circle guide](/blog/ultimate-golden-circle-guide/) covers everything you need to know about the surrounding area.

## Farm-to-Table Dining at Ylja Restaurant

What sets **Laugarás Lagoon** apart is Ylja Restaurant, led by renowned Icelandic chef Gísli Matt. The seasonal menu showcases the finest local ingredients sourced from nearby farms, fisheries, and greenhouses. Cap off your [geothermal bathing experience](/blog/top-5-hot-springs-in-iceland/) with an exceptional culinary journey celebrating Iceland's farm-to-table traditions. All ages are welcome to dine at Ylja Restaurant. A visit here fits seamlessly into our [Golden Circle Complete with Farm-to-Table](/private-day-tours-iceland/golden-circle-complete-farm-to-table/) private tour.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Entry admission with taxes.

**Requirements:** Minimum age 8 years for lagoon access (all ages welcome at restaurant), guests must bring swimwear and towel, shower required before entering pools.

**Good to Know:** Full refund if cancelled at least 1 day before. 2-hour duration. Wheelchair accessible with private changing rooms. Minutes from Golden Circle attractions. Food and drinks available at Ylja Restaurant. Book at least 30 minutes in advance.
