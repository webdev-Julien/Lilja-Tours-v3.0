## Europe's Most Powerful Hot Spring Powers Ultimate Wellness

**Krauma Geothermal Baths** stands as one of [Iceland's premier wellness destinations](/blog/top-5-hot-springs-in-iceland/), powered by Deildartunguhver: Europe's most powerful hot spring producing 180 liters per second. Located near Reykholt in [West Iceland](/multiday-tours-iceland/west-iceland-silver-circle/), this contemporary facility combines sustainable design with volcanic power. Modern architecture frames spectacular mountain panoramas through large windows and outdoor spaces.

Your 2-hour visit grants access to six pools with varying temperatures: warm relaxation pools, therapeutic hot pools, and cold plunge pools. Beyond pools, enjoy a Finnish sauna, two steam rooms, and a relaxation room with fireplace. Mineral-rich geothermal water connects you directly to Iceland's volcanic geology.

## Sustainable Luxury in West Iceland's Pristine Landscape

This **[geothermal spa](/partner-experiences-iceland/hot-springs/)** exemplifies sustainable tourism with zero carbon emissions from renewable energy. Expansive windows maximize natural light while offering mountain views. Winter creates magical contrasts between steaming pools and snow; summer's midnight sun illuminates evening sessions. The Borgarfjörður location provides easy access to [Hraunfossar](/blog/25-best-waterfalls-iceland/#hraunfossar) waterfalls, Reykholt, and Langjökull glacier — all highlights of the [Silver Circle](/blog/ultimate-guide-to-the-silver-circle/).

Krauma maintains intimate scale and tranquil atmosphere without overcrowding. Six different pool temperatures allow customized experiences—alternating hot and cold for therapeutic benefit, or finding your perfect temperature for contemplative soaking. Family-friendly policies welcome guests of all ages. Krauma fits perfectly into a [luxury wellness weekend](/blog/luxury-wellness-weekend-iceland/) or a [one-day Silver Circle itinerary](/blog/one-day-itineraries-silver-circle-iceland/).

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** 2-hour admission to Krauma Geothermal Baths, unlimited access to six geothermal pools with varying temperatures, traditional Finnish sauna access, two steam room facilities, relaxation room with fireplace, modern changing room facilities with secure lockers, shower amenities, towel provided.

**Requirements:** Swimwear required (available for purchase on-site), thorough shower before entering pools mandatory per Icelandic bathing customs, all ages welcome with family-friendly environment, independent transportation to Reykholt required, waterproof bags recommended for personal items.

**Good to Know:** Free cancellation up to 24 hours before visit. Year-round operation. Six pool temperatures from cold plunge to warm relaxation. Powered by Deildartunguhver (Europe's most powerful hot spring). Towels included. Panoramic mountain views. Sustainable facility with 100% renewable energy. Located near Hraunfossar waterfalls in West Iceland.
