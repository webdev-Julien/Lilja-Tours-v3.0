## From the City's Edge to the Ice

The **Glacier Expedition helicopter tour** is the quickest way to stand on an Icelandic ice cap. In 90 minutes from Reykjavík Domestic Airport you fly from the suburbs to the high-altitude silence of **Þórisjökull**, the glacier nearest the capital, and step out onto it for about 15 minutes before flying home a different way. It is the flight to choose among our [helicopter tours from Reykjavík](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-tours/) if touching the ice matters more to you than covering distance.

This is the shared version, priced per seat with up to four passengers and a window for everyone. The same expedition can be flown with the helicopter reserved for your group alone: see the [private Glacier Expedition](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-glacier-expedition-private/).

## The Route: Fjord, Waterfall, Glacier and Rift

The outbound leg follows **Hvalfjörður**, the Whale Fjord, past its vertical cliffs to the canyon at its head where **Glymur** waterfall drops 198 metres, visible in full only from the air or after a three-hour [hike](/private-day-tours-iceland/glymur-waterfall-hike/). Beyond the fjord the land rises and the colours simplify: dark jagged volcanic rock, then the clean white expanse of Þórisjökull. The pilot chooses the landing site according to the day's weather. On the ice the quiet is total.

The return traces the skyline of **Skjaldbreiður**, the broad shield volcano whose lava floors the Þingvellir valley, and crosses the **Þingvellir rift**, the fracture where the North American and Eurasian plates continue their slow separation. The contrast between the glacier and the fissured plain is the point of the route. Travellers who want more time on ice should look at our [Sólheimajökull glacier hike](/private-day-tours-iceland/solheimajokull-glacier-hike/) on the south coast, and our guide to the [best helicopter tours in Iceland](/blog/best-helicopter-tours/) explains how this flight compares with the longer expeditions.

## Weather, Flexibility and What to Expect

Sturdy hiking or winter boots are essential, since the landing is on snow or uneven mountain terrain, and warm layers are needed in any season: it is colder on the glacier than in town. The cabin is heated and large coats can travel in the baggage compartment. If the weather closes the planned route the pilot offers an alternative; if a short delay would help, the flight can be postponed by a few hours or to another day, subject to availability; and a full refund applies if the operator must cancel. Many of the ice images in our [helicopter photo gallery](/blog/best-pictures-helicopter-private-tours/) come from this landing.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Seat on a shared helicopter flight (up to 4 passengers), approximately 90 minutes of flight time, one remote scenic landing of about 15 minutes (weather permitting), experienced pilot with commentary, Bose noise-cancelling headsets.

**Requirements:** Sturdy hiking or winter boots for the glacier landing, several warm layers in any season, booking at least 2 hours before departure. Passenger weights are requested at booking for flight planning. Independent transfer to Reykjavík Domestic Airport, or ask us to include the flight in a private tour or transfer.

**Good to Know:** Flights operate year-round and depend on weather; alternative routes or another departure time are offered when conditions change, and a full refund applies if the operator cancels. The landing site is chosen by the pilot on the day. Departures from Reykjavík Domestic Airport, not Keflavík. Cancellation fees: 10% within 14 days, 50% within 7 days, 100% within 2 days. Price is per person; a private version is available for your group only.
