## Experience Raufarhólshellir - The Lava Tunnel Near Reykjavík

**The Lava Tunnel** (Raufarhólshellir) stands as one of Iceland's longest lava tubes, formed 5,200 years ago during a powerful eruption. Just 30 minutes from Reykjavík, it's one of the most accessible volcanic cave experiences in Iceland.

Professionally constructed paths and an elevated footbridge ensure safety throughout accessible sections. Impressive lighting highlights changing colors of volcanic rock and demonstrates the seismic forces that carved this passage.

## What Makes This Lava Tunnel Experience Special

This **guided tour** explores volcanic geology in a safe environment suitable for most fitness levels. Your guide provides insights into the eruption that created this tunnel and ongoing seismic activity defining Iceland's landscape.

Dramatic lighting reveals lava flow patterns, color variations, and volcanic scale. Tours depart hourly from 09:00 to 17:00—an excellent half-day activity complementing Golden Circle extensions.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Professional guided tour, safety helmet, lights, crampons if needed for winter conditions, entry and admission fee, access to constructed paths and footbridge through the tunnel.

**Requirements:** Suitable for most people, though not recommended for those with balance difficulties, problems walking on uneven surfaces such as snow, or difficulties with steep stairs. Warm clothes and sturdy shoes essential. Minimum fitness level required for navigating the tunnel's length.

**Good to Know:** Tours operate year-round with departures every hour from 09:00 to 17:00. Booking required at least 1 hour in advance. Cancellation policy enforces 100% fee if booking cancelled within 24 hours of tour. Duration approximately 50-60 minutes. Located just 30 minutes from Reykjavík for easy access.
