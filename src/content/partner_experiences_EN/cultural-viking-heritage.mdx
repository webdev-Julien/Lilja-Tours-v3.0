## Journey Into Viking Iceland

Experience the living history of Iceland's Viking settlers through immersive cultural activities and expert storytelling. This authentic heritage experience brings the sagas to life at a carefully reconstructed Viking settlement.

## Traditional Crafts and Ancient Skills

Witness traditional Viking crafts including textile weaving, woodworking, and metalwork. Learn about the ingenuity required to survive and thrive in Iceland's challenging environment using only period-appropriate tools and techniques.

## Norse Mythology and Saga Tales

Expert storytellers share captivating tales from Iceland's medieval sagas and Norse mythology, providing context for understanding how Viking culture shaped modern Icelandic identity and continues influencing the nation today.
