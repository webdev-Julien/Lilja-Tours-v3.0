## Extreme Volcanic Wilderness Expedition

**3-Hour ATV Adventure Near Reykjavík** delivers Iceland's most challenging [ATV experience](/partner-experiences-iceland/atv-snowmobile/) through Hengill volcanic range, where steaming fumaroles, bubbling [hot springs](/blog/top-5-hot-springs-in-iceland/), and multicolored mineral deposits paint the landscape. Technical river crossings, lava field navigation, and steep mountain ascents challenge experienced riders through terrain impossible to reach conventionally.

The Hengill system creates Iceland's largest high-temperature geothermal field with contrasting elements: snow-capped peaks above steaming valleys, cold streams intersecting with boiling springs. Expert guides lead small groups (maximum 8) through this dynamic environment ensuring safety while maximizing adventure.

**Panoramic glacier views** reveal Eyjafjallajökull and Mýrdalsjökull ice caps on the horizon, demonstrating Iceland's fire and ice duality. Guides contextualize these views within volcanic history and monitoring efforts. Our [ultimate Reykjanes guide](/blog/ultimate-reykjanes-guide/) covers the wider volcanic region surrounding the base area.

## Remote Wilderness Access

The **extended 3-hour duration** penetrates remote areas creating genuine wilderness expedition impossible on shorter tours. Physical demands require good fitness, but accomplishment proportionally increases.

**Small group size** (maximum 8) enables close oversight for technical sections, individual instruction, and appropriate pacing while minimizing environmental impact on sensitive geothermal areas.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** 3-hour extreme volcanic ATV expedition, ATV and fuel, expert extreme terrain guide, comprehensive safety briefing and advanced instruction, helmet with protective visor, waterproof overalls and gloves, ski mask and rain gear, access to Hengill volcanic range, technical river crossings, lava field navigation, panoramic glacier viewpoints, optional hotel pickup and drop-off (if selected).

**Requirements:** Valid driver's license required for ATV operators (international permits accepted), minimum age 17 years for drivers or 6 years for passengers, good physical fitness essential for demanding terrain and extended riding, previous ATV experience beneficial though expert instruction provided, warm clothing layers worn under provided overalls, sturdy waterproof hiking boots mandatory, independent transportation to base camp (15 minutes from Reykjavík) unless optional pickup selected, arrive 15 minutes before departure.

**Good to Know:** Seasonal operation May 15 - November 15 (Tuesday-Saturday only). Located 15 minutes from Reykjavík in Hengill volcanic range. Maximum 8 participants. Duration 3 hours riding (4.5 hours with pickup). Technical river crossings and lava field navigation. Valid driver's license required; passengers minimum age 6. Good fitness essential; previous ATV experience beneficial.
