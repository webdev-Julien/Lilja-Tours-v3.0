## The Volcano Flight, on Your Own Terms

The **private Reykjanes volcanoes helicopter flight** puts the whole aircraft at the disposal of your group, up to four passengers, for a 30 to 40 minute flight over the youngest landscape in Europe. It follows the same route as the [shared Reykjanes volcanoes flight](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-volcanic-eruption/), but with no strangers on board, a departure time arranged around your day and a pilot who can circle a crater twice if that is what you want. For a family or a group of four, the price per person is close to the shared seat; for a couple, it buys privacy.

Reykjanes is where the Mid-Atlantic Ridge comes ashore, which is why the ground keeps opening. Eruptions at **Fagradalsfjall** in 2021 and 2022 and along the **Sundhnúkur** fissure from late 2023 to 2026 have built crater rows, buried roads and pushed lava up against the barriers protecting Grindavík and the Blue Lagoon. Even when quiet, these fields are a reminder of the forces still at work beneath the crust.

## The Route: Craters, Fissures and Fresh Lava

From Reykjavík Domestic Airport the helicopter reaches the first lava within ten minutes. The pilot tracks the 2021 craters at Fagradalsfjall and the **Meradalir** valley filled by the 2022 flow, then swings toward the Sundhnúkur crater rows and the newest fields near Grindavík, where steam still rises from fissures and the rock is streaked rust and black. Flowing lava is never guaranteed; the volcano keeps its own schedule. The craters, fissures and new lava fields are spectacular in any season, and the pilot's commentary through the Bose headsets explains what you are looking at.

Pair the flight with a ground-level day on the peninsula: our [Reykjanes Peninsula private tour](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-peninsula/) visits Gunnuhver, Krýsuvík and the Bridge Between Continents, and the [Fagradalsfjall volcano hike](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-fagradalsfjall-hike/) walks to the 2021 crater. Background reading: the [recent volcanic history of Reykjanes](/blog/reykjanes-volcanic-recent-history/) and our complete [Reykjanes guide](/blog/ultimate-reykjanes-guide/).

## Weather, Flexibility and What to Expect

There is no landing on this flight, so ordinary shoes are fine, but warm layers are sensible in any season. The cabin is heated and large coats can travel in the baggage compartment. If weather closes the planned route the pilot offers an alternative; if a short delay would help, the flight can move by a few hours or to another day, subject to availability; and a full refund applies if the operator must cancel. Because the helicopter is yours, we can also time the flight to connect with an airport transfer or the end of a private day tour.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Private helicopter exclusively for your group (up to 4 passengers), approximately 30 to 40 minutes of flight time over the Reykjanes eruption sites, experienced pilot with commentary, Bose noise-cancelling headsets.

**Requirements:** Warm layers in any season, booking at least 2 hours before departure. Passenger weights are requested at booking for flight planning. Independent transfer to Reykjavík Domestic Airport, or ask us to include the flight in a private tour or transfer.

**Good to Know:** Flights operate year-round and depend on weather; alternative routes or another departure time are offered when conditions change, and a full refund applies if the operator cancels. Visible lava is never guaranteed, but the craters and new lava fields are spectacular in any season. Departures from Reykjavík Domestic Airport, not Keflavík. Cancellation fees: 10% within 14 days, 50% within 7 days, 100% within 2 days. Price is per helicopter; shared seats are available at a lower price per person.
