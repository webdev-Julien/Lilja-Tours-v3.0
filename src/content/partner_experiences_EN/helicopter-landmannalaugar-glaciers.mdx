## Fire, Ice, Water and Air in One Flight

The **Hot Springs and Glaciers private helicopter tour** is the flight we recommend when a group has a single afternoon to see the interior of Iceland. In 2.5 to 3 hours you cross the whole southern highland zone: steam rising from fissures in the crust, ice caps running to the horizon, and glacial rivers braiding across black plains. The helicopter is reserved for your party alone, up to four passengers, so the pilot can shape the route around what you most want to see. It is the longest of our [helicopter tours from Reykjavík](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-tours/) that still fits into half a day.

Departure is from Reykjavík Domestic Airport, a 10 to 15 minute drive from downtown. Within minutes of lifting off, the city gives way to lava fields and the first glacier appears ahead.

## The Route: Two Glacier Volcanoes and the Painted Mountains

The flight heads first to **Eyjafjallajökull**, the ice-capped volcano that grounded Europe's air traffic in 2010, then on to its larger neighbour **Mýrdalsjökull**, which hides the Katla volcano beneath several hundred metres of ice. Between the two lies **Þórsmörk**, a green valley hemmed in by glaciers and braided rivers that takes a full day to reach by [super jeep](/private-day-tours-iceland/thorsmork-highland-valley-hiking/). From the air you take it in at a glance.

North of the glaciers the colour changes. **Landmannalaugar** is a landscape of rhyolite mountains in ochre, rust, green and grey, cut by steaming streams and black obsidian lava. This is where you usually land for about 15 minutes, though the pilot chooses the exact spot each day according to weather and your preferences. The return leg passes **Hekla**, Iceland's most active volcano in historical times, and **Háifoss**, a 122-metre waterfall dropping into a volcanic canyon that few visitors ever see from this angle.

If you would rather spend a full day on the ground in the same area, our [private Landmannalaugar tour](/private-day-tours-iceland/landmannalaugar-highland-paradise/) is the road-based alternative. Both are described in our guide to the [best helicopter tours in Iceland](/blog/best-helicopter-tours/).

## Weather, Flexibility and What to Expect

Icelandic weather changes fast and safety decides everything. If conditions close the planned route, the pilot proposes an alternative; if a few hours' delay would help, the flight can be postponed the same day; and if the operator has to cancel without a rescheduling option, you receive a full refund. Bring sturdy boots, since landings are often on uneven ground or snow, and dress in warm layers. The cabin is heated and large coats can travel in the baggage compartment.

The route is the same one captured in our [helicopter photo gallery](/blog/best-pictures-helicopter-private-tours/), and it pairs naturally with a [luxury wellness weekend](/blog/luxury-wellness-weekend-iceland/) in Reykjavík.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Private helicopter exclusively for your group (up to 4 passengers), approximately 2.5 to 3 hours of flight time, one scenic landing of about 15 minutes (weather permitting), experienced pilot with commentary, Bose noise-cancelling headsets.

**Requirements:** Sturdy hiking or winter boots for the landing, several warm layers in any season, booking at least 2 hours before departure. Passenger weights are requested at booking for flight planning. Independent transfer to Reykjavík Domestic Airport, or ask us to include it in your private tour.

**Good to Know:** Flights operate year-round and depend on weather; alternative routes or a later departure are offered when conditions change, and a full refund applies if the operator cancels. Departures from Reykjavík Domestic Airport, not Keflavík. Cancellation fees: 10% within 14 days, 50% within 7 days, 100% within 2 days of the flight. Price is per helicopter, not per person. Book early for summer and for sunset slots.
