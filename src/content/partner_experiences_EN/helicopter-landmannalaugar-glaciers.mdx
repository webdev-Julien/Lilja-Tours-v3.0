## Ultimate Highlands & Glaciers Aerial Expedition

**Helicopter Tour to Landmannalaugar & Glaciers** delivers Iceland's most comprehensive aerial adventure, combining the country's most iconic glaciers, dramatic highlands, active volcanoes, and powerful waterfalls in a single spectacular 2.5-hour journey. This exclusive private helicopter tour departs from Reykjavík, following Iceland's dramatic south coast where powerful Atlantic waves meet black volcanic beaches. Your expert pilot navigates through mountain valleys to Þórsmörk, the legendary "Valley of Thor," where three glaciers converge creating one of Iceland's most beautiful wilderness areas accessible primarily by 4x4 or helicopter.

The expedition continues over Eyjafjallajökull glacier-volcano, infamous for its 2010 eruption that disrupted European air travel, revealing how massive ice caps conceal powerful volcanic systems. Adjacent Mýrdalsjökull glacier appears even more imposing, covering Katla volcano: one of Iceland's most powerful and closely monitored volcanic systems. From glacier-covered peaks, the flight transitions into Iceland's interior highlands, reaching the otherworldly Landmannalaugar geothermal area where rainbow-colored rhyolite mountains, steaming hot springs, and black obsidian lava flows create surreal landscape. A 15-minute landing allows you to step into this remote wilderness, experiencing the stark beauty and profound silence of Iceland's interior highlands.

## Landmannalaugar Landing and Return via Hekla

The **Landmannalaugar landing** provides rare opportunity to experience Iceland's interior highlands without the challenging 4x4 journey required for ground access. During your 15-minute stopover, you'll stand surrounded by multicolored mountains painted in vibrant orange, red, yellow, and green hues created by rhyolite volcanic deposits and geothermal activity. The contrast between vivid mountain colors, black lava fields, and white snow patches creates photographer's paradise. Steam rises from natural hot springs throughout the valley, demonstrating the geothermal forces that continue shaping this dynamic landscape.

Your pilot shares insights into the area's geology, explaining how different volcanic rock types create the rainbow mountains, how geothermal heat fuels the numerous hot springs that make Landmannalaugar famous among hikers, and why this region serves as the starting point for Iceland's legendary Laugavegur trekking trail. The landing transforms abstract aerial views into tangible connection with Iceland's highland wilderness—experiencing the remoteness, silence, and raw beauty that make these interior regions so compelling.

## Hekla Volcano and Háifoss Waterfall

The **return flight to Reykjavík** passes Hekla volcano, one of Iceland's most active volcanic systems with eruptions recorded throughout Icelandic history. Medieval Europeans called Hekla "Gateway to Hell," believing its eruptions signaled divine punishment. Your pilot explains Hekla's eruption patterns, its significance in Icelandic geology and culture, and ongoing monitoring efforts tracking volcanic activity. The aerial perspective reveals Hekla's elongated fissure shape and the extensive lava fields surrounding its base—evidence of centuries of volcanic activity.

The **Háifoss waterfall** provides dramatic finale to your aerial adventure. At 122 meters, Háifoss ranks among Iceland's highest waterfalls, plunging into a narrow gorge carved through volcanic rock. The helicopter's aerial perspective reveals both the waterfall's impressive height and the dramatic canyon it has carved over millennia. Adjacent Granni waterfall creates twin cascade effect, doubling the visual impact. The combination of powerful waterfalls, deep canyon, and surrounding volcanic landscape demonstrates the erosive forces continuously reshaping Iceland's terrain.

This efficiency makes the tour particularly valuable for travelers with limited time seeking maximum exposure to Iceland's diverse landscapes, or those preferring aerial perspectives and comfort over strenuous ground-based adventures.
