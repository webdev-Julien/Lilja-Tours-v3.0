## Ultimate Highlands & Glaciers Aerial Expedition

**[Helicopter Tour](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-tours/) to [Landmannalaugar](/private-day-tours-iceland/landmannalaugar-highland-paradise/) & Glaciers** combines glaciers, dramatic highlands, [active volcanoes](/partner-experiences-iceland/helicopter-volcanic-eruption/), and waterfalls in a 2.5-hour journey. From Reykjavík, fly through [Þórsmörk valley](/private-day-tours-iceland/thorsmork-highland-valley-hiking/), over Eyjafjallajökull and Mýrdalsjökull glaciers, to [Landmannalaugar](/private-day-tours-iceland/landmannalaugar-highland-paradise/)'s rainbow-colored rhyolite mountains where a 15-minute landing lets you experience this remote wilderness.

## Landmannalaugar Landing and Return via Hekla

The **landing** provides rare highland access without challenging 4x4 journey. Stand surrounded by multicolored mountains in vibrant orange, red, yellow, and green created by rhyolite deposits. Steam rises from hot springs demonstrating geothermal forces shaping this landscape.

## Hekla Volcano and Háifoss Waterfall

The **return flight** passes Hekla volcano—medieval Europeans called it "Gateway to Hell." The aerial perspective reveals its elongated fissure shape and surrounding lava fields.

**Háifoss waterfall** provides dramatic finale—at 122 meters, one of Iceland's highest, plunging into a gorge alongside adjacent Granni waterfall.

## Exclusive Private Experience

This **private tour** accommodates maximum 4 passengers and personalized commentary. The comprehensive route condenses [multiple days of highland driving](/blog/7-day-highland-camping-expedition/) into one spectacular aerial journey.
