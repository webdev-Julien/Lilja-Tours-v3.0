## Iceland's Most Dramatic Oceanside Infinity Pool

**[Sky Lagoon](/partner-experiences-iceland/sky-lagoon-sky-pass/) Pure Pass** represents Iceland's boldest geothermal wellness innovation: a stunning infinity pool where warm waters appear to merge with the Atlantic Ocean. Located minutes from Reykjavík, this contemporary facility reimagines ancient bathing traditions through modern design. The infinity edge creates the illusion of floating in waters extending endlessly toward the ocean.

The Pure Pass provides 2-3 hours of unlimited access to the 70-meter lagoon maintained at 38-40°C. Mineral-rich geothermal water flows from deep volcanic sources. Admission includes towel service and contemporary changing facilities at an affordable price point.

## Contemporary Design Meets Ancient Geology

The **Sky Lagoon** architecture honors geothermal heritage while embracing contemporary design. The facility integrates with volcanic rock and coastal terrain, using natural materials and viewing areas framing spectacular [Reykjanes panoramas](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-peninsula/).

Soak while watching waves crash against ancient lava formations, feeling contrast between geothermal warmth and crisp ocean breezes. Winter offers northern lights possibilities; summer brings magical midnight sun bathing. Minimum age 12 years makes it ideal for families with older children, couples, or solo wellness seekers.

## Strategic Location for Maximum Convenience

**Sky Lagoon's position** just 15 minutes from Reykjavík provides exceptional convenience, easy accessibility by car, taxi, or public transport. Perfect for arrival day relaxation, pre-departure unwinding, or [wellness breaks ](/blog/luxury-wellness-weekend-iceland) during Reykjavík visits. The Reykjanes Peninsula features volcanic landscapes and the Bridge Between Continents.

The Pure Pass provides the essential Sky Lagoon experience without premium additions. Modern changing facilities include secure lockers; towel service eliminates packing concerns. The dramatic coastal setting creates memories that endure.

## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Sky Lagoon Pure Pass admission for 2-3 hours, unlimited access to the 70-meter oceanside infinity lagoon, geothermal waters maintained at 38-40°C, towel provided, modern changing room facilities with secure lockers, shower amenities.

**Requirements:** Minimum age 12 years, swimwear required (available for purchase on-site if needed), thorough shower before entering lagoon mandatory per Icelandic bathing customs, independent transportation to Sky Lagoon required (car, taxi, or public bus available), waterproof bags recommended for personal items.

**Good to Know:** Year-round operation. Infinity pool with seamless Atlantic Ocean views. Waters maintained at 38-40°C. Winter northern lights viewing possible; summer offers midnight sun bathing. Located 15 minutes from Reykjavík, close to Keflavík Airport. Minimum age 12. Towel included. Pure Pass provides essential experience; Sky Pass upgrade available with Skjól ritual.
