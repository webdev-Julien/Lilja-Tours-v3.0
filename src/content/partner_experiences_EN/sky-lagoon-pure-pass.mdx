## Reykjavík's Oceanside Lagoon, Ritual Included

**Sky Lagoon Saman Pass** is the classic admission to Iceland's most striking geothermal lagoon: a 70-metre infinity edge where warm, mineral-rich water appears to pour straight into the North Atlantic. The Saman Pass now includes the complete seven-step Skjól ritual, so the only thing separating it from the [Sér Pass](/partner-experiences-iceland/sky-lagoon-sky-pass/) is the changing room. Sky Lagoon consistently ranks among the [best hot springs in Iceland](/blog/top-5-hot-springs-in-iceland/).

"Saman" means "together" in Icelandic. You change in well-kept shared facilities with individual shower cabins, collect your towel and wristband (which works as locker key and payment card), then walk out into the lagoon. From there the whole site is yours for as long as you like: the swim-up bar, the cave section, the ritual, and the view.

## The Skjól Ritual in Brief

The ritual unfolds in a turf-roofed building beside the lagoon and takes around 45 minutes at a relaxed pace. **Laug**: start in the 38-40°C lagoon. **Kuldi**: a cold plunge at about 5°C. **Ylur**: a sauna whose glass wall frames the ocean. **Súld**: a walk through cold mist. **Mýkt**: Sky Lagoon's signature body scrub. **Gufa**: a steam room at around 46°C. **Saft**: a shot of Icelandic crowberry juice before you return to the water.

The alternation of hot and cold is the point. Icelanders have bathed this way for generations, and most guests find the second soak in the lagoon feels completely different from the first.

## Location and Timing

Sky Lagoon sits at the tip of Kársnes in Kópavogur, a 13-minute drive from downtown Reykjavík and 45 minutes from Keflavík Airport, with free parking. That makes it a natural finish to an arrival day, a [Reykjanes Peninsula tour](/private-day-tours-iceland/reykjanes-peninsula/) or a [wellness weekend](/blog/luxury-wellness-weekend-iceland/) in the capital. Late slots in winter bring a chance of northern lights above the water; midsummer evenings mean bathing under a sun that never sets. ## Practical Information

**What's Included:** Sky Lagoon Saman Pass admission, unlimited access to the 70-metre oceanside infinity lagoon at 38-40°C, the complete seven-step Skjól ritual (lagoon, cold plunge, sauna, cold mist, body scrub, steam room, crowberry drink), towel, shared changing facilities with individual shower cabins, locker, shampoo and conditioner, wristband for cashless payments at the lagoon bar.

**Requirements:** Minimum age 12 years; ages 12-14 must be accompanied by an adult. Swimwear required (rental available on site). A thorough shower before entering the lagoon is mandatory, as at all Icelandic pools. Independent transportation required (car, taxi or city bus routes 4, 35 and 36); we can include the visit in a private tour or transfer on request.

**Good to Know:** Open year-round; hours vary by season, from 10:00-21:00 in winter to 8:00-23:00 around midsummer. Located at Kársnes, Kópavogur, 13 minutes from central Reykjavík, with free parking. Minimum age 12; ages 12-14 must be accompanied by an adult. Allow 2-3 hours. Towel, shampoo and conditioner provided; swimwear rental available on site. Upgrade to the Sér Pass for a private changing room. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your visit.
